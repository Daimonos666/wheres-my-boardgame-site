Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email wheresmyboardgame@outlook.com. A screenshot helps.

How is storage organised?

A Location is any place you keep games: a room, the attic, a storage unit. A Container is the storage in it: a Kallax shelf, a cabinet, a box of cards. The exact spot is up to you, for example Column 1, Row 2. Use them however suits your collection.

How do I import my BoardGameGeek collection?

On boardgamegeek.com, export your collection as a CSV file and save it to your phone (the Files app). In the app, open Settings, Collection, Import from BoardGameGeek, and pick the file. You see everything before it is saved.

My collection does not show up on my other device.

Sync uses your own iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID and that iCloud is on for the app. The first sync of a large collection can take a few minutes. Settings, iCloud, shows the current status.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data on that device. Data in iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.
