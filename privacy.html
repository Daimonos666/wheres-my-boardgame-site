Privacy Policy for Where's My Boardgame

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Where's My Boardgame is a personal inventory app. It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data, and has no account, advertising or analytics.

What the app stores

The games, locations, containers, notes, details and settings you enter are stored on your device in the app's own database.

Importing

If you import a BoardGameGeek collection file, the app reads it on your device to add your games. The file is not sent anywhere and is not kept.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app mirrors your data to your private iCloud (CloudKit) storage so it syncs between your own devices. This data is held by Apple under your Apple ID. The developer cannot access it. You can turn it off in iOS Settings under your Apple ID, iCloud.

What the app does not do

It does not use your camera, photos, location, contacts or microphone, does not connect to the internet on its own, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the on-device data. Data in iCloud can be removed in iOS Settings under iCloud, Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes it will be updated on this page.
